# Tiffin Wallah — Pause, Monthly Payment, Addresses & Referral Upgrade

## Service pause
- Paid Monthly Tiffin and Press Wallah orders can request a pause.
- Minimum pause duration is 3 days. A 1–2 day pause is rejected.
- Customer enters start/end dates from Account > Orders & Payments.
- The service pauses immediately for the requested range and the request appears in Admin > Pause Requests.
- Admin can approve or reject the request.
- A single pause is limited to 31 days.

## Monthly payment window
- Admin settings contain a configurable payment window.
- Default is the 1st through 5th of every month.
- The customer-facing Tiffin page displays this rule.

## Referral offer
- Default referral code: `TWFREEPRESS`
- Admin can enable/disable and change the code and offer text.
- A valid code used on a monthly tiffin order gives the customer a free Press Wallah offer.
- Claiming the offer creates one free Press Wallah credit.
- The credit can be selected during a Press Wallah booking; the resulting booking is confirmed without a Razorpay charge.
