# Tiffin Wallah — requested service upgrade

## Customer side
- Removed daily tiffin ordering. Tiffin ordering is now monthly only.
- Added two monthly tiffin plans: Standard and Premium.
- Added admin-controlled pre-booking switch and offer text.
- Pre-booking offers unlock after the configured claim period (default 15 days) and expire after the configured validity period (default 2 months).
- Added customer-side offer status and Claim Offer action in Account.
- Party service now exposes only Standard Party and Premium Party packages. Custom Party is removed.
- Added public Rules Book page and navigation link.
- Renamed Laundry customer-facing branding to Press Wallah while keeping washing/pressing options.
- Added service allocation IDs after successful payment:
  - Standard Tiffin: TW 0001, TW 0002...
  - Premium Tiffin: TW 0001-P, TW 0002-P...
  - Press Wallah/Laundry: LW 0001, LW 0002...
  - Party: PW 0001, PW 0002...
- Allocation numbers are generated only after payment verification.

## Admin side
- Added three service dashboards:
  - Standard Tiffin Dashboard
  - Premium Tiffin Dashboard
  - Press Wallah Dashboard
- Paid customer name, phone and address are shown directly in the service dashboard for fulfilment.
- Added Press Wallah price management.
- Added pre-booking offer management.
- Added Rules Book management.
- Existing customer removal, review editing and unpaid-payment reminder functionality remains.

## Branding
- Added a new scalable TW logo at `client/public/tw-logo.svg`.
- Existing overall visual language and navbar styling are preserved; the navbar labels were updated only where required by the requested services.

## Important
- The output package intentionally does not contain `server/.env` or real credentials. Copy `server/.env.example` to `server/.env` and add your own credentials.

## Advanced service / delivery upgrade
- Removed Integral University from the Contact block; operational Tiffin and Press Wallah addresses are now shown as map links.
- Added separate Standard/Premium weekly meal menus for Monthly Tiffin.
- Added separate Standard/Premium party menus with package-specific item pricing.
- Added Admin → Party Service section.
- Fixed Rules Book with API fallback to the menu endpoint.
- Added delivery partner model, env-seeded primary credentials, admin-generated partner IDs, assignment, and delivery dashboard.
- Delivery partners can only access assigned paid Monthly Tiffin and Press Wallah orders.
- Added mobile-first dashboard/card layouts for customers, admin, and delivery partners.
- Logo in the footer is now a home link.
